A little help goes a long way

The coast has one old promise:
nobody gets left on the tide.

Your tug is the newest boat to carry it. A stranded sailboat needs a tow. Then a fishing boat. Soon the whole harbor is counting on your next clean turn.

Three corners of the coast

Sunwake. Copperdock.
Lantern Strait.

Start in bright, sheltered water. Learn the rhythm of a working waterfront. Finish under the amber harbor lamps. One simple drag control carries you through the whole coast.

Earn skipper XP, strengthen your tug and rope, tackle daily goals, and unlock twenty illustrated achievements. Local saves keep your progress close at hand.

Built for a quick cast-off

Android first. Portrait controls, readable touch targets and local offline progression.

Browser friendly. A Godot Web build with local progress and graceful Android-service fallbacks.

Open source. Godot 4.5.1 Compatibility renderer, Kenney models and original music, with preserved third-party notices.
