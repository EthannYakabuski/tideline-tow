# Tideline Tow store pack

Prepared for the first prototype review. No store submission has been made.

- `app-icon.png`: 512 × 512 full-bleed app icon.
- `feature-graphic.png`: 1024 × 500 feature illustration.
- `splash.png`: portrait launch artwork, separate from gameplay evidence.
- `store.json`, `short-description.txt`, `long-description.txt`: listing copy, category, proposed tags, lore and three selling cards.
- `achievements.json` and `achievements/`: 20 named achievements and unique 512 × 512 illustrations.
- `leaderboards.json` and `leaderboards/`: three larger-is-better board definitions and unique 512 × 512 illustrations.
- `screenshots/phone`, `screenshots/tablet7`, `screenshots/tablet10`: actual runtime captures. Read `../docs/SCREENSHOTS.md` for sizes, provenance and limits.
- `source/`: nine original generated source illustrations, including six four-icon atlases.
- `art-prompts.json`: exact prompts and generation method.

Generated illustrations are promotional artwork, not screenshots. All 23 icon motifs are distinct; no numbers or text are baked into their art. Runtime icons are 128 × 128 copies under `assets/brand`.

The long description deliberately discloses test advertising and incomplete production Play Games setup. Update that paragraph only after the production services are configured and tested. Suggested tags are not a claim that each label appears in the current Play Console.

Before submission, the owner must complete support contact, public privacy URL, content rating, target audience, Data safety, ads declaration, production service/signing configuration and final device testing. No placeholder backend ID may be uploaded as a real ID.
