Little tug. Big rescue.

A boat needs a hand. Then another. Soon your little tug is pulling a whole colorful convoy through the harbor.

Tideline Tow is a portrait harbor-rescue arcade game built around one satisfying job: bring every boat home. Drag to steer, pick up stranded boats, and guide your growing tow chain back to the safe harbor. A longer convoy earns a bigger delivery, but needs more room to turn. Take the bold route, or bank your rescue before the next crossing?

THREE COASTS TO CALL HOME
Explore sunny Sunwake Cove, the working waterfront of Copperdock, and the amber lights of Lantern Strait. Thirty authored contracts introduce rocks, moving ferries and currents in compact rescue challenges. Learn a route, protect your hull, and return for a better star rating.

ONE THUMB, A WHOLE LITTLE FLEET
Steer with a simple drag control. Boats join your rope when you get close; the harbor banks your convoy when you return. Your three hull hearts make every tight turn matter.

A REASON TO CAST OFF AGAIN
Try Endless Watch for a new personal best, or take on the day's seeded rescue. Daily goals and seven-day welcome gifts help your skipper grow. Earn XP, improve rope capacity and hull protection, and collect twenty illustrated achievements.

YOUR PROGRESS STAYS WITH YOU
Campaign progress, upgrades and personal records are saved locally. The core game works offline. Internet access is needed for optional advertising and online services when available.

Advertising appears at natural breaks: a small harbor/results banner, an interstitial after every third completed round when available, and an optional rewarded continue after a failed rescue. A rewarded continue is granted only after the ad confirms completion. Browser play keeps local progress, with no simulated ad rewards.

Set a course. Gather your fleet. Bring them home.
