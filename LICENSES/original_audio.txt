KENNEY SOUND EFFECTS
The seven .ogg effects in assets/audio are unmodified files from Kenney's Interface
Sounds and Impact Sounds packs. These are CC0-1.0, not Apache-licensed original music.
Their exact original filenames and byte hashes are recorded in effects_manifest.json.
The original pack notices are preserved in kenney_interface-sounds.txt and
kenney_impact-sounds.txt alongside this file. Creator: Kenney (https://kenney.nl).

Testing note: PCM metadata, peak/RMS levels, full-file forward loop cues and hashes
were checked programmatically. This does not establish physical-device listening
quality or the final in-game music/effects balance; those require human listening.
